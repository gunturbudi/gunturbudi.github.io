Collaboration with the **Public Health Department, Universitas Gadjah Mada** (2021).

[F1000 Research Page](https://f1000research.com/articles/10-983)

TOMO is a mobile application designed to improve treatment adherence for drug-resistant tuberculosis (TB) patients. The app provides medication reminders, side-effect tracking, and communication channels with healthcare providers.

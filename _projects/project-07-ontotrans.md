Funded by the **European Union's Horizon 2020** research and innovation programme under Grant Agreement No 862136 (2023).

[Project Website](https://ontotrans.eu/)

OntoTrans develops an ontology-driven open translation environment for materials modelling and simulation, enabling interoperability across different modelling and simulation tools.

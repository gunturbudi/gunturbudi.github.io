Funded by **ASEA-UNINET**, 2025.

This project conducts a comparative and harmonization analysis of three major data protection regulations: the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), and Indonesia's UU-PDP (Personal Data Protection Law).

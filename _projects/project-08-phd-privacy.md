Doctoral Research Project, **Universität Wien** (2020–2024).

[ResearchGate page](https://www.researchgate.net/project/An-Intelligent-Systems-Approach-for-Supporting-Privacy-Awareness-in-Agile-Software-Development)

Developed methods and tools based on machine learning and NLP to help software developers integrate privacy considerations into requirements and design processes in agile development. Key outcomes include PrivacyStory (tool for extracting privacy requirements from user stories), NER-based privacy detection, and learning-to-rank frameworks for privacy design patterns.

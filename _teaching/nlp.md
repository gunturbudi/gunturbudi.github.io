**Level:** Graduate (Master/PhD)
**Department:** Computer Science and Electronics, Universitas Gadjah Mada

This course covers advanced topics in natural language processing including neural architectures (transformers, BERT, GPT), large language models, fine-tuning strategies, prompt engineering, and NLP applications in information extraction, question answering, and text generation.
